# BASE WORLD MALAWI — Founding Charter

**Motto:** From Malawi to the World.  
**Supreme principle:** Humanity Before Technology.  
**Commitment:** Innovation Without Limits. Responsibility Without Compromise.

## Mission
Research, develop and responsibly deploy secure, useful and accessible technology
that solves real problems and improves human life.

## Vision
Contribute original, measurable technological innovation from Malawi to the world.

## Ten founding laws
1. Innovation — investigate, invent and improve rather than merely imitate.
2. Humanity first — protect life, dignity and human rights.
3. Excellence — test quality, reliability and accessibility.
4. Security — protect systems, accounts and information.
5. Honesty — communicate capabilities and limitations truthfully.
6. Equality — design for inclusion and non-discrimination.
7. Responsible intelligence — use AI and science safely and transparently.
8. Accountability — document decisions and take responsibility.
9. Global vision — build locally with international standards.
10. Legacy — create sustainable value for future generations.

This is a founding working charter, not a registered legal constitution.
Formal governance and registration must follow applicable Malawi law.
